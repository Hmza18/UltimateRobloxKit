# Visual Pattern Matrix

One row per reusable pattern. The evidence for each row's behaviour is tagged in the "Observed behaviour" column:

- **(D)** Documented: a source states it. See the game file for the citation.
- **(O)** Observed: a guide or wiki describes how it looks.
- **(A)** Assumed: inference or unverified general knowledge of the genre. Most timing and animation rows are (A); confirm against footage before quoting them as fact.

Game short names: BF Blox Fruits, TF Throw Food For Animals, PS99 and PSX the two pet simulators, TAP tapping simulators, AM Adopt Me!, BSS Bee Swarm Simulator, GAG Grow a Garden, SAB Steal a Brainrot, ANI anime simulators, DOC Roblox documentation, DF DevForum practice. Game files are in [games/](games/pet-simulator-99.md); platform facts are in [roblox-platform-notes.md](roblox-platform-notes.md).

UVK modules are those of [the architecture document](../../architecture/visuals.md). All timings are estimates.

## Game comparison

Every cell is an assessment, not a measurement. Cells marked (A) rest on unverified general knowledge of the game; cells marked (D) are supported by a cited source in the game file.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Throw Food For Animals | Low to medium: meter, luck, details button (D) | Bright, cartoon (A) | Medium: rarity and mutation on arrival (A) | May follow the projectile (A) | Animals placed in a base with income (D) | Not documented |
